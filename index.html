01 Problem

Why I built it

Orenburg is my hometown and one of Russia's main grain regions. Most warehouses there check grain by hand sampling, and imported monitoring systems have become harder and more expensive to get. I wanted a probe a small farm can afford and install by hand.

5.7–7.8%

of stored grain eaten by insects within 6 months · VNIIZ

grain lost to storage pests in Russia per year, upper estimate · Rosselkhoznadzor

04 Machine learning

Random Forest · scikit-learn · my own recordings

93.4%

Accuracy on held-out data · 267 / 286 windows

  • 4 classes · silence, single knocks, repeated impulses, airborne noise
  • Insect stand-in · metronome (repeated impulses), 106 / 106 correct
  • 12 features · level, spread, peaks, autocorrelation
  • Window · 20 amplitudes, step 5
  • Split · first 70% of each recording to train, last 30% to test
